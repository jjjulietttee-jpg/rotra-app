Common questions

  • Restore a subscription: Settings → Rotra Pro → Restore purchases, or Restore on the subscription screen.
  • Change the schedule or reminder: Settings → Your schedule.
  • Skip a site in the rotation: Sites → tap the site → Skip this site.
  • Move to a new iPhone: your journal travels with your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer).
  • Cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rotra.

Rotra is a personal log, not medical advice.
