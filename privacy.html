Privacy Policy

Rotra: GLP-1 Shot Tracker · Last updated September 25, 2026

Rotra is a personal journal for GLP-1 shots. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your entries, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Shots you log: date and time, injection site, the dose label you type, medication name and notes.
  • Side effects you note, their strength, your weight, protein and water entries.
  • Your schedule, targets and settings.

This health information is stored only in Rotra's own storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Rotra reads your body weight to show your trend and can save weights you log back to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with third parties and never leaves your device through Rotra. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Reminders

Shot-day reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone for the day and time you chose. No push server is involved.

Report for your clinician

The PDF report is created on your iPhone. It is shared only if you choose to share it, with whoever you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Rotra receives only the status of your subscription, never your payment details. We may use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate subscriptions; it does not receive any of your health entries.

Analytics and tracking

Rotra does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete Rotra from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Not medical advice

Rotra is a personal log. It does not provide medical advice, does not calculate or recommend doses and does not diagnose. Talk to your clinician about your treatment.

Children

Rotra is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
